New `HtxInverted` (exchange): the two HTX flames in white and blue `#008CD6`, taken from htx.com's dark-theme header logo, for dark backgrounds where the default's navy `#00003E` flame vanishes. `<ExchangeIcon name="htx" variant="Inverted" />` renders it, the manifest's `Htx` entry lists `'Inverted'` among its `variants`, and the Iconify set gains `exchange-htx-inverted`.

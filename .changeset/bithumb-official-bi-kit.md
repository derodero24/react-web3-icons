**Visual change: `Bithumb` is now the current Bithumb symbol** from Bithumb's official CI·BI page (bithumbcorp.com). It is the same b-and-flag mark, now with the official paths and the Bithumb Orange gradient: the b and flag run from `#FF6C00` to `#DC3700`, and the stem from `#FF6C00` to `#FF9100`. In 4.0.0 the b was a flat `#F47320`, and the flag `#D53127` with a red-to-orange shadow on the b's shoulder. `BithumbMono` is now the logo package's one-colour symbol, whose flag stands apart from the stem. The manifest's `brandColor` is now Bithumb Orange, `#ff6c00` (was `#d53127`).
